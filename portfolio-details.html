Autonomous Document Search & Synthesis

NexMutation designed and deployed a specialized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform indexing over 500,000 regulatory filings, audit records, and internal compliance protocols.

By employing hybrid vector-keyword retrieval and re-ranking, the solution reduced research query response times from 4 hours down to under 5 seconds with zero hallucination guarantee through strict citation verification.
