Privacy Policy

Our commitment to data confidentiality, transparent AI, and strict privacy protection.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

1. Introduction & Commitment

At NexMutation Technology ("NexMutation", "we", "our", or "us"), we design and deploy state-of-the-art Artificial Intelligence, machine learning systems, and data analytics architectures for enterprise organizations. We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting confidential client data, personal data, and proprietary intellectual property.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, handle, and protect data when you interact with our website (nexmutation.com), submit inquiries through our contact channels, or engage our engineering team for consulting and custom software development.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is strictly necessary to communicate with you and provide our technology services:

  • Contact Inquiries: When you submit our website contact form or email us directly, we collect your name, business email address, message subject, and inquiry details.
  • Client Project Data: Any datasets, documentation, or technical artifacts shared with us under formal client contracts are governed by strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and dedicated data security protocols.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: We may collect non-identifiable technical parameters such as browser type, operating system, and general access metrics to monitor server health and uptime.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate your technical requirements and respond to your direct inquiries.
  • To deliver, maintain, and optimize custom AI models, RAG pipelines, and automated workflows.
  • To uphold contractual obligations and deliver ongoing support care.
  • We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or company information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Enterprise AI Data Safety & Non-Training Guarantee

A core principle of NexMutation is safe, responsible artificial intelligence:

  • No Public Model Leakage: Client proprietary documents, internal knowledge bases, and private data pipelines deployed via our RAG and LLM solutions are isolated in private tenant architectures.
  • Model Training Isolation: Your private data is never used to train public commercial AI models without explicit, written customer authorization.

5. GDPR & Global Privacy Standards Compliance

For visitors and partners operating in jurisdictions protected by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or equivalent privacy statutes, NexMutation upholds the following fundamental rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") of your contact records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You may object to the processing of your data at any time.

6. Data Security & Encryption

We implement robust organizational and technical safeguards, including transport-layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access controls, and secure development lifecycles to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
